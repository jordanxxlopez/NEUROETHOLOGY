# Lecture 68 paper access

All eleven selected primary papers were downloaded as valid PDFs. No instructor uploads are required. The ten content papers cover MRI anatomy, ear micro-CT, dung chemistry and discrimination, contact and courtship calls, acoustic individuality, vocal networks, and juvenile communication. The eleventh supplies the original published title-slide rhinoceros photograph.

Bibliographic metadata was verified through Crossref and compared with the downloaded papers. Print issue years are used for online-first articles. Publisher access failures were resolved through the Rhino Resource Center academic archive; no figure substitutions were made.

See REFERENCES.md for full references and source_access.json for successful PDF locations. PDFs remain in the ignored papers directory; original crop coordinates are recorded in crops.json.
