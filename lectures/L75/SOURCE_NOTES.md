# Scientific distinctions retained

- Ahrens (2012) functional maps combine sequential recordings and animals; they are not simultaneous measurements of every neuron in one brain.
- Ahrens (2013) reports spontaneous whole-brain activity, not virtual-reality behavior. Its alternating-network interpretation is identified as a hypothesis.
- Portugues (2014) distinguishes optokinetic eye tracking from optomotor swimming and separates visual-field combinations and behavioral regressors.
- Mu (2019) supports radial-astrocyte participation using ablation, pharmacology and optical activation. Partial ablation reduced passivity rather than abolishing it. The molecular signal from glia to GABAergic neurons and the relevant GABA receptor subtype were not established.
- Source-authored experimental schematics remain inside published panels. None was drawn, reconstructed, recolored or generated for this deck. Shared axes are retained by cropping complete panel groups.
