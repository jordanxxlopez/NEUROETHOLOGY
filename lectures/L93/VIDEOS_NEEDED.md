2. **Movie S2**, `science.adi8081_movie_s2.mp4`: RAmVOC-TeLC mice do not produce the elicited cries while escape responses persist. Proposed slide 33 beside S1. Source: https://www.science.org/doi/suppl/10.1126/science.adi8081/suppl_file/science.adi8081_movie_s2.mp4
