## Evidence limits

Right-GI2 photoablation **increases**, rather than decreases, left-turning tendency under the reported lateral-wind test. Electrical replacement reduces that enlargement. Tethered turning tendency is a leg-derived index, not body angle. Juvenile receptive fields in Booth et al. are predictions, not new recordings. Candidate ion-channel contributions in Olsen and Triblehorn and calcium signaling in Jankowska et al. remain proposed explanations. Arena locomotion after injury does not establish restored predator avoidance.
