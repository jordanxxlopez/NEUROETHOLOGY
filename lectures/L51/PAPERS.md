# Lecture 51 — verified primary sources

All 14 selected source PDFs are available and analyzed. The four foundational papers supplied by the instructor resolved the earlier download gate. The fifth upload, Jacobs et al. (2016; published online 2015), contains original newborn elephant cortex data despite naming the giraffe in its title. This replaces the proposed standalone adult morphology source; no unavailable paper is used as a figure source. Adult comparisons shown in the uploaded article are identified as comparisons.

4. Joshua M. Plotnik, Richard Lair, Wirot Suphachoksahakun, Frans B. M. de Waal (2011). Elephants know when they need a helping trunk in a cooperative task. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 108: 5116-5121. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1101765108

5. Karen McComb, Cynthia Moss, Sarah M. Durant, Lucy Baker, Soila Sayialel (2001). Matriarchs As Repositories of Social Knowledge in African Elephants. Science 292: 491-494. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1057895
