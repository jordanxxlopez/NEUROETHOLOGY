# Lecture 77 article-video audit

Every cited study was checked. New recordings must be supplied or explicitly cited by a primary academic article; related primary studies are allowed. YouTube, YouTube-backed embeds, unrelated web videos and reconstructed recordings are excluded.

| Original study | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Harlow (1958), DOI 10.1037/h0047884 | The supplied paper explicitly says a 20-minute film illustrated the fear, open-field, Butler-box and retention behaviors. No downloadable, verified copy of that article's film was obtained. The BYU-Idaho result was a YouTube-backed Kaltura entry, so it was excluded. Other results were web excerpts, commentary or stock footage. This does not mean the original film does not exist. |
| Yamaguchi et al. (2012), DOI 10.1038/ncomms2088 | Supplementary Movie 1 obtained from the original publisher; retained on slide 7. |

Related robot studies were checked; no obtainable, verified article recording was selected. |
