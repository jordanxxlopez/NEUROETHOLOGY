# Lecture 32 — verified sources

Exact title: Vocal communication I: songbird song-system anatomy — HVC–RA–Area X (Nottebohm)

Exact date: Monday, November 9, 2026

All four requested sources were supplied. Nottebohm, Benezra, and Gale/Perkel were original article PDFs. The Bottjer upload was a text-only licensed reprint; its findings were checked against that text and its bibliographic record, and it contributed no images. Nine additional PDFs were downloaded before the uploads, and Maxwell et al. (2021) supplied additional original syrinx anatomy. No required PDF remains outstanding.

Figures come from ten primary research articles. Gale and Perkel’s 2010 anatomical review supplies supporting definitions only; its images were not used. The two Gale/Perkel 2010 publications are disambiguated as 2010a (primary study) and 2010b (review).
