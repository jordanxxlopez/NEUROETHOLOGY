# Lecture 84 videos in Google Slides

The PPTX contains real embedded MP4 movies. Google Slides can drop PowerPoint embedded movies during import, retaining only their poster images.

Upload the MP4 files in media/ to Google Drive. On the corresponding imported slide, choose Insert > Video > Google Drive and select the matching file. Place it over the existing poster, keeping its proportions. Do not replace the separate article figure.
