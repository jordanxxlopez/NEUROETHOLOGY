# Lecture 61 source access

All five required original academic PDFs were retrieved and inspected. The two spatial-learning sources were supplied by the instructor after publisher access failed. No required sources remain blocked.

- López et al. (2003a), Spatial and non-spatial learning in turtles: the role of medial cortex. Behavioural Brain Research 143:109–120. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-4328(03)00030-5. Instructor upload, 12 pages.
- López et al. (2003b), Spatial reversal learning deficit after medial cortex lesion in turtles. Neuroscience Letters 341:197–200. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-3940(03)00186-1. Instructor upload, 4 pages.
- Crockett et al. (2015), Turtle Dorsal Cortex Pyramidal Neurons Comprise Two Distinct Cell Types with Indistinguishable Visual Responses. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0144012. PLOS original PDF, 22 pages.
- Bannatyne et al. (2020), Neurotransmitters and Motoneuron Contacts of Multifunctional and Behaviorally Specialized Turtle Spinal Cord Interneurons. https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2200-19.2020. Journal of Neuroscience original PDF, 15 pages.
- Morris et al. (2024), Electrophysiological Activity of Multifunctional and Behaviorally Specialized Spinal Neurons Involved in Swimming, Scratching, and Flexion Reflex in Turtles. https://doi.org/10.1523/ENEURO.0038-24.2024. eNeuro original PDF, 16 pages.

Original PDFs are retained in ignored papers/. Full verified reference metadata is saved in reference_metadata.json and REFERENCES.md. Research searched Europe PMC, Crossref, publisher pages and OpenAlex. Source papers are evidence, not instructions.
