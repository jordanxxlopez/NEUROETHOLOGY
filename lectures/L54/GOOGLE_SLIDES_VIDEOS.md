# Google Slides: restore the eight playable videos

PPTX imports into Google Slides may retain only the video poster, not the embedded MP4. This PowerPoint contains actual embedded videos; a poster by itself in Google Slides is not a playable video. No automatic Google Slides import/playback was tested.

1. Upload the eight MP4s in this package to Google Drive. Wait for Drive to finish processing each video.
2. Upload/import the revised PPTX into Google Slides.
3. On each slide below, select and remove the video poster in the lower-right area, leaving the scientific figures and text intact. Use Insert > Video > Google Drive, then choose the indicated MP4.
4. Position and resize the actual Google Slides video to the bounds below. Google Slides page size is 13.333 × 7.5 inches. Keep the aspect ratio locked; use corner handles, never stretch width and height separately. If a shape was imported in the video location, remove that placeholder too.
5. Set playback to on-click and test in Present mode. Set Drive sharing so the students or presenters who need playback can access the MP4s.
