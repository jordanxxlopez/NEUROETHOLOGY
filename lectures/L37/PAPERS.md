2. Hafting T, Fyhn M, Molden S, Moser MB, Moser EI (2005). Microstructure of a spatial map in the entorhinal cortex. Nature 436(7052): 801–806. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature03721

3. Giocomo LM, Hussaini SA, Zheng F, Kandel ER, Moser MB, Moser EI (2011). Grid cells use HCN1 channels for spatial scaling. Cell 147(5): 1159–1170. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2011.08.051

Figures are original PDF crops. The title uses the published rat-and-platform drawing in O'Keefe and Dostrovsky Fig. 1; it is not a created image. The early hippocampal recordings are taught with their sensory, directional and state conditions intact. The HCN1 study is a forebrain intervention, not a grid-cell-specific knockout. Source introductions and discussions are identified as proposed mechanisms rather than direct measurements.
