# Lecture 7 primary sources

All 13 primary PDFs are available. The instructor supplied the three foundational papers after publisher downloads were blocked. No PDF remains outstanding.

Europe PMC scholarly web searches and Crossref records were checked against the downloaded or uploaded full articles, including author order, journal, volume, pages/article identifier, year and DOI. PDF hashes and local paths are in sources.json; papers are ignored by Git. Original figure pixels, labels and source colors are preserved.
