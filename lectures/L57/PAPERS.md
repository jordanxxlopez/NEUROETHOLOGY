# Lecture 57 scientific sources

All 11 required PDFs are available and verified. The instructor supplied Patel, Schachner, Bendor and Wang, and Snowdon; the other seven were downloaded from academic sources. No uploads are outstanding. PDFs remain in scratch storage and are not committed. Citation metadata, acquisition provenance and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in research.json. Original panel crops and exact source pages are recorded in crops.json and figure_sources.json.
