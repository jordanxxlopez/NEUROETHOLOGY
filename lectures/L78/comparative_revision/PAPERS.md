# PDFs required for the comparative Lecture 78 revision

The prior deck is preserved. The new 44-content-slide plan gives rats only three slides and covers multiple additional animals. These full published papers are needed for original figure crops and final scientific verification. No replacement PPTX has been built.

1. **Dagher et al. (2026). Comparative analysis of tickling and conspecific play in tame mice and golden hamsters.** Behavioural Brain Research 496:115849. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbr.2025.115849).
2. **Demuru et al. (2025). Tackling Hominin Tickling: Bonobos Share the Social Features and Developmental Dynamics of Play Tickling With Humans.** American Journal of Primatology 87:e23723. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajp.23723).
3. **Maglieri et al. (2020). Levelling playing field: synchronization and rapid facial mimicry in dog-horse play.** Behavioural Processes 174:104104. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.beproc.2020.104104).
4. **Maglieri et al. (2024). Smiling underwater: Exploring playful signals and rapid mimicry in bottlenose dolphins.** iScience 27:110966. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2024.110966).
5. **Schwing et al. (2017). Positive emotional contagion in a New Zealand parrot.** Current Biology 27:R213–R214. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2017.02.020).

The 2024 author preprint of the mouse/hamster study was downloaded for preliminary research. The final published 2026 paper is still required; its figures and final results will not be silently replaced with preprint material. Full-text XML is available for the bonobo and dolphin papers, but their PDFs remain unavailable.
