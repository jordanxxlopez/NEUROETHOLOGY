Original source URLs, article DOI, credits, transformations and media hashes are in VIDEOS.json and speaker notes. The eight Jokura MP4s are embedded byte-for-byte. Burkhardt S1 is remuxed with the H.264 stream copied unchanged. The two Moroz clips use source intervals 8–37 seconds, retain 640 × 480 resolution and original frame rate, and are converted with lossless H.264 and lossless ALAC audio. Decoded video-frame hashes match the original source intervals exactly. No recoloring, speed change, new graphics, generated frames or lossy size reduction was applied.

The Jokura high-speed clips retain the authors 0.3× playback speed. It is part of the original recording, not a lecture-created speed edit. Sources without audio remain silent. Native PowerPoint playback on macOS and Windows has not been tested in this cloud environment; file structure, embedded hashes, decoding and static rendering are validated separately.
