# Evidence review notes

- The 2022 Pong article uses a responsiveness-based operational definition of sentience. Welfare discussions in this deck explicitly use subjective experience and distinguish the two definitions.
- The main-results text gives a predictable-feedback duration of 10 ms; STAR Methods gives 100 ms. The deck teaches their shared 100-Hz frequency and omits this inconsistent duration. Unpredictable feedback is 5 Hz for 4 s in both descriptions.
- Functional connectivity is inferred from activity relationships. It is not a direct count of anatomical synapses.
- The reduced-network Figure 4 analysis reports changes in weight, modularity, clustering, and betweenness but no reliable characteristic-path-length change. Slide 30 preserves that distinction.
- K252a is described by Cai et al. as a plasticity-blocking intervention. The deck attributes its use to that experiment without claiming molecular selectivity established by this result.
- The 2026 cybernetic framework is a perspective, not primary Doom performance data. Its Doom mention refers to the 2022 Pong study. No Doom learning numbers or consciousness claims are inferred from it.
- Ethical proposals in Milford et al. are taught as proposals, including their assumptions about valence and experience. They are not presented as measured welfare thresholds.
- All figures are unmodified crops from original published article pages. Framework diagrams are the authors' published panels; no diagram or graph was created for this deck.
