# NEUR 411 Neuroethology — Fall 2026

This repo builds the course's lecture PowerPoints.

- **To make any lecture, use the `neuroethology-lecture` skill** (`.claude/skills/neuroethology-lecture/SKILL.md`). It holds every format rule; follow it without waiting to be reminded.
- Lectures 8, 9 and 10 are the approved model (46 slides: title + 44 content + key takeaways). Lectures 1-7 are an older format — do not copy them. Their closing "does not establish" caveat paragraphs predate the Oct 9 rule; do not copy that habit.
- **Never create images.** Figures come from published articles (cropped panels, `Author (year), Fig. N`, DOI) — these get priority. Nearly every slide carries an image: at least 40 of 44 content slides, at least 34 with primary-article figures. The title slide shows the study animal (paper figure or credited web photo). Any slide about a specific structure (brain region, neuron, sense organ) carries an anatomy image of it. Credited web images (`Photo: …`, credit, license; neuroscience-related is fine) only where needed, max 10. Prefer colorful figures (fluorescence/stained micrographs, color maps and plots, color animal photos) over grayscale — color on at least half of the image slides, color only from the source, never recolored; backgrounds unchanged. No schematics, diagrams, re-plotted charts, illustrations or AI images. If a PDF can't be obtained, ask the instructor to upload it; otherwise use a text slide. **Exception — Unit 4 special topics (lectures 41+, date "Date TBD", listed under `special_topics` in `course/schedule.json`):** don't stop for PDFs; use what is accessible, swap in another accessible primary paper when one is blocked, and ask only if a truly foundational paper is unavailable.
- **Useful article videos:** apply to new lectures and existing decks being updated. Check supplements and article-provided recordings; embed useful original media in the PPTX when supported, with author/year, exact movie identifier or filename, DOI, original URL, credit and supplied license. Sources must be publisher/article media or an author/lab repository explicitly linked or cited by the article. **Never YouTube**, unrelated web videos, generated animation or reconstructed footage. Compatibility conversion to MP4 is allowed without changing scene, timing, audio or scientific content. Verify saved media and playback when possible, retain a credited static fallback, and distinguish static exports from the complete embedded-video PPTX. If unavailable, explain briefly, provide the original link/file where possible and continue. No quota; preserve titles, slide counts and figure requirements. Full rule 11 in AGENTS.md and the skill.
- **Writing:** every sentence teaches source content — no roadmap/transition/figure-reading/takeaway framing, no 'this shows/illustrates', no attention-directing phrases, no filler; speaker notes are a natural teaching transcript; concepts over statistics (no p-values, test names, ± errors); no math the sources don't show. Every paragraph teaches; a limitation gets a sentence only when it changes what students should conclude, not on every slide (no closing "does not establish" paragraphs). No fixed paragraph structure. No em dashes in slide text. Full rules in the skill; enforced by `tools/style_rules.py`.
- Lecture titles and dates live in `course/schedule.json`. Never change a title. Special topics (Unit 4, lectures 41+) go under `special_topics`, titled in the same style, date "Date TBD"; they replace a scheduled lecture only when the instructor chooses.
- Color themes: `course/themes.json` — never reuse one, no yellow/orange, no purple/green. Titles and bold text are a darker shade of the title-slide color; all other text is black (`tools/theme_colors.py`, enforced by the checker).
- Tools: `tools/theme_colors.py` (re-align a finished deck's colors), `tools/build_lecture.py` (spec → .pptx, then checks), `tools/check_lecture.py` (rule checker for any deck), `tools/crop_figure.py` (cut figure panels from paper PDFs).
- Python deps: `pip install -r requirements.txt` (python-pptx, Pillow) and poppler (`pdftoppm`).
- `AGENTS.md` holds the same rules for Codex; keep it in sync with the skill.
