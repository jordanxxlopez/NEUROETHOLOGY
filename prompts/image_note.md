# Image note (paste into any lecture prompt)

```text
Images — on nearly every slide
Almost every slide should have a figure, not just a minimum number; ignore any "at least 15/18
figures" requirement. At least 40 of the 44 content slides carry an image, and most of those are
figures cropped from the primary papers. The title slide shows the animal the lecture is about,
so students know what it looks like; this can be a figure from a primary paper or a credited web
photo. When a slide discusses a specific brain region, neuron, sense organ or other structure,
include an image of it, from an article if possible, otherwise a credited web image. A few
credited web images, including neuroscience-related ones, are fine where they help, at most 10
per deck. Prefer colorful figures (fluorescence and
stained micrographs, color-coded maps, heat maps, color plots, color photos of the animal) over
grayscale ones, aiming for color on at least half of the image slides; the color must come from
the source, never recolored or edited, and backgrounds stay as they are. Never create, draw or
generate any image.
```
